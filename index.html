01 / ABOUT US

Who we are

Freezers Esport is a Czechoslovak CS2 team built on players with clearly defined roles — from entering the site to lurking to the final clutch. The team is led by feitmenn, who serves as team captain.

We believe discipline in practice carries over into matches — that's why we train roles, not just aim. The goal is steady progress in tournaments and gradually building a name in the scene.

  • You'll get a 2-week trial period to play within the team — scrims, FACEIT matches, and voice comms included.
